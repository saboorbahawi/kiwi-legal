Privacy Policy

Kiwi Technology · Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Kiwi Technology ("Kiwi", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Kiwi Technology mobile app and related services. Kiwi Technology is located in Barchi, Kabul, Afghanistan.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name and email address. If you choose Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication receives basic Google profile information such as your name, email address, and the profile photo URL provided by Google. We use this information to sign you in and maintain your account. We do not request access to Google contacts, Drive, or other Google account data.
  • Order information: the name, phone number, and delivery address you enter at checkout; items, prices, selected payment method, order status, and order date.
  • App content: administrators may upload product, service, payment-method, and announcement images and related descriptions for display in the app.
  • Technical information: Firebase and other providers may process IP addresses, user-agent/device and app information, and service logs to operate the app, protect accounts, diagnose issues, and prevent abuse.

How we use information

We use account information to create and secure accounts; order information to receive, confirm, and fulfill orders and respond to questions; and technical information to operate and protect the app. We use administrator-submitted images and descriptions to display app content. We do not use Google account data for advertising, sell it, or use it for unrelated purposes.

Where information is stored and who processes it

Account, profile, and order information is stored using Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database. Administrator-uploaded images are handled by ImageKit. Google provides sign-in when you choose that option. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies: Firebase privacy information, Google Privacy Policy, and ImageKit Privacy Policy. Information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live.

How we share information

We make order details available to authorized Kiwi administrators so they can manage and fulfill orders. We share information with the service providers listed above as needed to operate the app, with your direction or consent, or if required by law or needed to protect the app and its users. We do not sell personal information or share Google user data for advertising.

Payments

The app records the payment method selected and the payment status of an order. It does not currently collect payment-card numbers or online banking credentials in the checkout form. Follow the payment instructions shown in the app and do not send sensitive payment credentials through order fields.

Retention and your choices

We keep account and order information for as long as needed to operate the service, manage orders, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Order records may be retained after an account is removed when needed for business or legal purposes. You may request access to or correction or deletion of your account information by contacting us. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing a request.

Security

We use Firebase security rules and service-provider safeguards intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

The app is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. We will post the revised version here and update its effective date.
